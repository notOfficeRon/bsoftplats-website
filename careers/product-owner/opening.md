title: Product Owner
description: We are looking for a Product Owner to join our team. The ideal candidate has experience building user-facing applications within the financial services domain and understands how to bridge business needs with technical execution.
responsibilities:
- Act as the primary point of contact between stakeholders and the development team, ensuring alignment on priorities and scope.
- Collect, analyse, and prioritise requirements from business and technical stakeholders.
- Translate requirements into a clear and actionable scope of work, including user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Manage and maintain the product backlog, ensuring it reflects business priorities and technical feasibility.
- Lead sprint planning, backlog refinement, and other Agile ceremonies, using Jira to manage workflows and track progress.
- Ensure delivery aligns with business goals, regulatory requirements, and customer expectations.
- Collaborate closely with developers, designers, QA, and business teams to deliver high-quality features on time.
requirements:
- Proven experience as a Product Owner, Business Analyst, or similar role in software delivery.
- Strong background in building user-facing applications, ideally mobile.
- Experience in the banking services domain is a strong advantage.
- Solid understanding of Agile methodologies and hands-on experience with Jira (or similar tools).
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate complex requirements into actionable tasks.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
nice to have:
- Experience working in regulated industries (banking, fintech, insurance, etc.).
- Familiarity with APIs and technical integrations.
- Knowledge of mobile UX/UI best practices.
